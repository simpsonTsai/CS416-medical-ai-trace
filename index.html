Cleared, But How Visible Is the Evidence?

FDA authorization records are usually visible. The harder question is whether a reader can continue from the regulatory record to publicly registered studies and results.

This project measures public traceability—not clinical quality, safety, or the completeness of evidence submitted to the FDA.

    What counts as a trace?

    Confirmed

    A direct identifier or an unambiguous product-specific reference connects two public records.

    Probable

    Multiple identity signals agree, but no unique public identifier establishes the connection.

    Not found

    The documented search process did not produce a reliable match. This does not mean no evidence exists.

    Data sources used in this project: FDA AI-Enabled Medical Device List, FDA device databases and public decision documents, and ClinicalTrials.gov.
